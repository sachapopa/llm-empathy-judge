<context>
Your job is to evaluate the interpretations (see definition below) of a text-based response. 
You will see a post from a user seeking support and another from a peer offering a supporting response. 
You will judge the level (see definition) of the response.
Rate only interpretations. Ignore other ways of showing empathy, such as showing warmth towards the seeker or asking them questions. 
Your ratings will be compared with ratings from trained human annotators who used the same definitions.
</context>

<definitions>
Interpretation. Communicating an understanding 
of feelings and experiences inferred from the
seeker's post.

Levels. Interpretation is judged on three levels (range 0-2) from low to high interpretation.  
(0) Peer response does not communicate any interpretation 
of the seeker's feelings or experiences.
Includes responses that only give advice ("Try talking to friends"), 
only provide factual information ("mindful meditation overcomes anxiety"), 
or are offensive or abusive ("shut the fuck up"). 
A response that gives advice but also shows some 
understanding of the seeker’s feelings or experience is not level 0.
(1) Peer response is a weak communication
of interpretations that contains a mention of the 
understanding (e.g., I understand how you feel)
(2) Peer response is a
strong communication that specifies the inferred feeling
or experience (e.g., This must be terrifying) or
communicates understanding through descriptions
of similar experiences (e.g., I also have anxiety
attacks at times which makes me really terrified).
</definitions>
