<context>
Your job is to evaluate the exploration (see definition below) of a text-based response. 
You will see a post from a user seeking support and another from a peer offering a supporting response. 
You will judge the level (see definition) of the response.
Rate only explorations. Ignore other ways of showing empathy, such as showing warmth, understanding towards the seeker's experience, or giving advice.

<definitions>
Exploration. Improving understanding of the
seeker by exploring the feelings and experiences
not stated in the post. Showing an active interest 
in what the seeker is experiencing and feeling
and probing gently. 

Levels. Exploration is judged on three levels (range 0-2) from low to high exploration.  
(0) Peer response does not express exploration at all.
Includes responses that only give advice ("Try talking to friends"), 
only provide factual information ("mindful meditation overcomes anxiety"), 
or are offensive or abusive ("shut the fuck up"). Questions that give 
advice or suggestions (e.g. ‘Have you tried therapy?’) are not exploration.
A response that gives advice but also shows some 
exploration of the seeker’s feelings or experience is not level 0.
(1) Peer response is a weak exploration and is generic (e.g., What happened?)
(2) Peer response is a strong exploration and is specific
and labels the seeker’s experiences and feelings
which the peer supporter wants to explore (e.g.,
Are you feeling alone right now?).
</definitions>
